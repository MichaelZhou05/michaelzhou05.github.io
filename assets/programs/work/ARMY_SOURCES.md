## Related published abstract

https://journals.gmu.edu/jssr/article/view/3436

Reinforcement Learning Based Channel Hopping: Implementation on Nano Drones. Authors: Ziqiang Zhou and Dr. Kai Zeng. Journal of Student-Scientists' Research, Vol. 4 (2022), published 2022-12-13. DOI: 10.13021/jssr2022.3436.

The supplied publication is GMU ASSIP work predating the 2025 Army internship. The page labels it as related earlier work and does not attribute its publication date to the Army role. The abstract compares UCB1, EXP3, and epsilon-greedy and reports the 95.3% UCB1 result for a stationary jammer in a lab environment; it is not evidence for dynamic-jammer or safety-constraint claims.
