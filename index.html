nordschleife eval

          

            Two frontier models were sat down in front of a hand-drawn Nürburgring and told to drive it.
            Neither is good at this. Take the keyboard and see if you are better.
          


          

            
              
              how this works — and why it exists
            
            

              

                This is not the real Nordschleife. It is a sketch of one: 84 control points pushed through a
                spline, following the real circuit's order of named corners and rescaled so the centreline adds
                up to the real 20.832 km. The corner shapes are drawn by eye, not surveyed, and the road is far
                wider than the real 8–9 m of asphalt, because at the zoom a 20 km lap needs, a true-width
                road is a hairline. Treat it as a caricature of the Ring, not a map of it.
              

              

                You drive it one sector at a time — clear the window you are in and it shifts to the next
                stretch of the Ring. Grass slows you down; the treeline is a wall.
              

              

                Neither model is a scripted pace car. Both lines are recordings of laps
                claude-opus-5 and gpt-5.6-sol xhigh actually drove. Each was shown the sector
                window the same way the page draws it for you — the road as a polyline, its car inside it,
                the gate it has to reach — and replied with arrow-key programmes. Those key presses ran
                through race-sim.js, the same physics function your keyboard drives. Same four keys,
                same grass penalty, same treeline.
              

              

                Inspired by GBA Eval, which
                grades models by publishing what they built and letting you play it instead of asking you to
                trust a score. Same idea at a much smaller scale: every number here is backed by a run you can
                watch, and you can regenerate either lap yourself with npm run opus-lap.
